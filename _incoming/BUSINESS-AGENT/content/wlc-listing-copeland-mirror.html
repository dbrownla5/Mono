Full Product Description

Vintage Greg Copeland layered mirror wall sculpture with a gold-tone frame and geometric, multi-dimensional Op Art design. This is a signed studio piece from the 1970s — part of the era when Copeland's layered mirror sculptures defined the intersection of fine art and functional décor in high-end American interiors.

The sculpture features multiple layers of cut and etched mirror set at varying depths within a structured gold-tone metal frame, creating the signature three-dimensional optical illusion that made Copeland's work iconic. The layered construction catches and refracts light from every angle, producing a shifting visual effect that changes depending on the viewer's position and the room's lighting. This is not a flat decorative mirror — it is a sculptural wall piece with real depth and presence.

Greg Copeland Studio pieces from the 1970s are actively collected by mid-century modern and Op Art enthusiasts. His work has sold through major design platforms including 1stDibs (where gallery-priced Copeland pieces average $1,600 and range up to $4,500+), LiveAuctioneers, and specialty vintage dealers. This piece is in pre-owned good condition with the kind of honest patina and character that confirms its age and authenticity.

Measures approximately 26 × 26 × 3 inches. Weighs approximately 30 pounds; boxed/shipping weight approximately 50 pounds. This is a substantial, large-format wall sculpture — it makes a statement. Local pickup strongly preferred due to weight, fragility of layered mirror construction, and shipping costs. Will ship with full professional packing (rigid box, mirror-safe corner protection, layered cushioning) at buyer's expense if needed.

Seller Notes & Red Flags

Shipping Risk

Layered mirror construction means multiple glass surfaces at different depths. One hard impact can crack an interior layer that's not visible until unpacking. Professional packing is non-negotiable. If shipping, build the cost into the listing price or pass it to the buyer explicitly. Local pickup eliminates this risk entirely.

Weight

At 30 lbs actual / 50 lbs boxed, this piece hits oversized surcharges on most carriers. UPS and FedEx Ground are the most cost-effective for heavy wall art. USPS is not an option at this weight. Get a shipping quote before committing to a price that includes shipping.

Condition Accuracy

List as "Pre-owned — Good." Be specific in photos and description about any patina, oxidation on the gold-tone frame, or clouding on any mirror layer. Copeland collectors expect honest vintage condition — they want authentic pieces, not restored ones. Photograph in natural light from multiple angles to show the layered depth effect.
