## Yelp Business Bio

The Well Lived Citizen is a concierge service for people navigating life's transitions — the ones you see coming and the ones that blindside you. We handle the chaos so you can focus on what matters.

Most people hire five different services: an organizer, a tech person, a resale consultant, a mover, a cleaner. We do all of it. We show up, assess what needs to happen, and execute across the whole picture. We organize your space, set up your tech, photograph and price your pieces, haul the resale inventory, and handle the logistics nobody else wants to touch.

We work with individuals, families, and estates. Whether you're downsizing after a move, inheriting a lifetime of accumulated items, managing a parent's transition, or simply ready to refresh your space, we provide clarity and care to move through it without the overwhelm.

**What we actually do:**

Home organization and modern moves. Legacy planning and inventory management. House calls for in-home consultations — we're the person who stops to pick up bags of clothes for resale after organizing your space, then stays to set up your mom's phone, Alexa, and TV remote while you're at work. Curated resale and consignment for pieces you don't have time to post, get a quote on, or know if anyone would even buy.

Our approach is straightforward: we listen, we organize, we execute. No judgment. No pressure. Just practical solutions for real transitions.
