# CLAIMS LEDGER — conflicts logged, never asked one at a time

This is where the system records **conflicting claims** found across repos —
especially money-level ones (pricing, commission, terms). The rule:

- **We do NOT interrupt Dayna to adjudicate a conflict mid-extraction.**
- Every version of a claim gets logged here with its **source and date**.
- When the same topic collects multiple claims, the picture assembles itself, and
  Dayna decides **once, from the whole ledger** — not 37 times, repo by repo.
- **No document wins by calling itself "final," "master," or "source of truth."**
  Those are labels, not verdicts. Everything here is a claim until Dayna says.

Columns: **Topic · Claim · Source (repo / file) · Date · Notes**
