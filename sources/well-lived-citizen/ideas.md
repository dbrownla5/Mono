# The Well Lived Citizen — Design Brainstorm

## Context
Brand: private household stewardship, Los Angeles. Audience: affluent adult daughters, emotionally intelligent professionals, independent older adults. Voice: observant, grounded, capable, calming, quietly high-standard, human. NOT: Pinterest organizer, minimalism cult, declutter culture.

**Design Movement:** New American Editorial — the feeling of a well-funded independent magazine meets a trusted local service. Think *Kinfolk* meets *The Strategist* meets a very good therapist's waiting room.

**Core Principles:**
1. Opaque color blocks as structural elements — not decoration, but architecture
2. Asymmetric text placement that feels deliberate, not random
3. Shadow as depth signal — box shadows on cards, text shadows on hero, drop shadows on color blocks
4. Type does the heavy lifting — weight contrast between display and body creates hierarchy without clutter

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: warm cream `#F5F0E8` — not white, not beige, something lived-in
- Ink: near-black `#1C1917` — warm, not cold
- Espresso block: `#2C2420` — dark, opaque, used as section dividers and card underlays
- Sage underlay: `#C8D5CC` — muted green, used as opaque block behind key statements
- Rust accent: `#B85C38` — terracotta, used sparingly for CTAs and active states
- Rationale: the palette should feel like a well-kept home, not a spa or a startup

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Full-bleed color blocks that stack vertically with deliberate contrast
- Text anchored left on most sections, occasionally centered for pull quotes
- Service cards use opaque dark underlays with light text — not glass, not gradient, just solid
- Homepage hero: large left-aligned headline, color block underlay behind key phrase, CTA below

**Signature Elements:**
1. Opaque espresso or sage rectangle behind key phrases — like a physical label or tag
2. Numbered service items with oversized numerals in muted color
3. Thin horizontal rules as section separators — not dividers, just breath

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Hover: subtle underlay appears behind text links (not underline, but a color block)
- CTAs: solid fill, no rounded corners, transform scale on active
- Nav: fixed, cream background, no blur — just solid and present

**Animation:**
- Entrance: fade-up on scroll, 200ms, ease-out — not dramatic, just present
- Stagger: 60ms between list items
- No parallax, no scroll-jacking — the content is the experience

**Typography System:**
- Display: Plus Jakarta Sans 700/800 — large, confident, no-nonsense
- Body: Plus Jakarta Sans 400/300 — readable, warm, not clinical
- Eyebrow: Plus Jakarta Sans 500, 0.65rem, tracked wide, uppercase
- Pull quotes: Plus Jakarta Sans 300 italic, larger size
- No serif — the brand voice is grounded and modern, not literary

**Design Movement:** West Coast Modernism — the visual language of a thoughtful Los Angeles practice. Warm, structured, human-scaled.

**Core Principles:**
1. Structural color blocks that define zones, not decorate them
2. Generous whitespace between sections — breathing room is trust
3. Shadow used on opaque underlays to create physical depth
4. Human photography (warm, intimate, lived-in) as the emotional anchor

**Color Philosophy:**
- Cream `#F5F0E8`, Near-black `#1C1917`, Sage `#C2D0C8`, Rust `#B85C38`, Warm gray `#8A8078`
- The rust is used only for CTAs and active states — it earns its place

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Homepage: stacked full-bleed sections, alternating cream and espresso backgrounds
- Service pages: left-rail navigation on desktop, full-width sections
- Opaque color block underlays behind key statements — not a highlight, a frame

**Signature Elements:**
1. Opaque dark rectangle behind hero headline — like a physical label
2. Service tiles: solid espresso background, cream text, no border radius
3. Pull quotes in sage block with white text

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Everything feels physical — buttons press, cards lift slightly, underlays appear
- No animations that feel like software — everything feels like print come to life

**Animation:**
- Scroll-triggered fade-up, 180ms ease-out
- Button: scale(0.97) on active, 120ms

**Typography System:**
- Plus Jakarta Sans 800 for display headlines
- Plus Jakarta Sans 400 for body
- Plus Jakarta Sans 500 tracked for eyebrows

## CHOSEN APPROACH: West Coast Modernism (Response 3)

Stacked full-bleed sections. Opaque color blocks as frames, not decoration. Rust CTAs. Espresso and cream alternating. Plus Jakarta Sans throughout. Shadow gives depth. Human voice in the copy. No rounded corners. No gradients. No blur effects on nav — solid and present.

This is the design that earns trust before a word is read.
