# Design Ideas — The Well Lived Citizen

## Approach A — Editorial Warmth
**Design Movement:** Warm editorial minimalism, like a high-end lifestyle magazine printed on cream stock.
**Core Principles:** Generous white space, restrained color, type-led hierarchy, nothing decorative that doesn't earn its place.
**Color Philosophy:** Cream background (#F5F0E8), charcoal ink (#231e1a), rust accent (#8B4A2F), warm linen (#D4C9B8). Colors feel like worn leather and natural linen — earned, not chosen.
**Layout Paradigm:** Asymmetric editorial columns. Text anchored left. Service pillars in a staggered grid. No centered hero banners.
**Signature Elements:** Thin horizontal rules as section dividers. The hanger logo used sparingly. Generous leading in body copy.
**Interaction Philosophy:** Quiet. Hover states reveal, not distract. No popups. No aggressive CTAs.
**Animation:** Subtle fade-in on scroll. Nothing faster than 300ms. Nothing that calls attention to itself.
**Typography System:** Plus Jakarta Sans (as required). Display: 400 weight, tracked wide. Body: 300 weight, generous line-height. Hierarchy built through size and spacing, not weight.
**Probability:** 0.07

## Approach B — Structured Luxury
**Design Movement:** Architectural precision meets tactile warmth — think a well-organized estate sale catalog from a curator, not a liquidator.
**Core Principles:** Grid discipline, typographic confidence, restraint over decoration, service-first layout.
**Color Philosophy:** Same palette as brand — cream, charcoal, rust, linen — but used with higher contrast. Rust as a true accent, not a wash.
**Layout Paradigm:** Vertical rhythm with clear zones. Full-width section breaks. Services presented as a structured list with pricing visible, not hidden.
**Signature Elements:** Thin vertical rules separating columns. Section labels in small caps. Price presented plainly, without apology.
**Interaction Philosophy:** Functional. Every click has a clear destination. Contact form is front and center, not buried.
**Animation:** None beyond subtle fade. Speed over spectacle.
**Typography System:** Plus Jakarta Sans throughout. Headers at 500 weight. Body at 300. Small caps for labels and navigation.
**Probability:** 0.08

---

## Approach C — Intimate & Narrative
**Design Movement:** Personal essay meets service directory — the site reads like a letter, not a brochure.
**Core Principles:** Voice-first, story-led, service details emerge from narrative, not the other way around.
**Color Philosophy:** Cream and charcoal dominant. Rust used only for links and key moments. The site should feel like reading something handwritten on good paper.
**Layout Paradigm:** Single-column narrative on About and Home. Services break into a clean grid only when listing specifics. Contact is conversational.
**Signature Elements:** Pull quotes from Dayna's own voice set large and left-aligned. The origin story is the hero — not a banner image.
**Interaction Philosophy:** Slow and deliberate. The site rewards reading. No carousels, no sliders.
**Animation:** Page transitions fade gently. Nothing bounces.
**Typography System:** Plus Jakarta Sans. Body text at a generous 18px. Headers restrained — the copy does the work, not the size.
**Probability:** 0.06

## Selected Approach: A — Editorial Warmth

The brand is about transitions — moving, letting go, starting over. The site should feel like the best version of that process: calm, organized, warm, and in capable hands. Editorial warmth gives Dayna's voice room to breathe without competing with design noise. It is elevated without being cold, and personal without being precious.

Design constants applied to all files:
- Font: Plus Jakarta Sans (Google Fonts)
- Background: #F5F0E8 (cream)
- Charcoal: #231e1a
- Rust: #8B4A2F
- Linen: #D4C9B8
- Ink (secondary text): #4a3f3a
- NO rounded corners on structural elements
- NO box shadows
- NO bubbles or pill shapes
- Thin 1px rules for dividers
- Asymmetric layouts, text anchored left
- Generous line-height (1.7–1.9 for body)
