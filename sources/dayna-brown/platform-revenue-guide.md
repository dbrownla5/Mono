## Tier 1 — Fastest Path to Income (This Week)

### TaskRabbit
**What it is:** On-demand local service marketplace. Clients post tasks, Taskers bid. Home organization is one of the top categories in LA.

**Why it works for you:** No waiting for SEO. Clients are actively searching and ready to hire. You set your own rate. You can be live and bookable within 24–48 hours of completing registration.

**Rate to set:** $85–$125/hr for home organization tasks. This is below your full-service rate intentionally — TaskRabbit clients are often one-time, smaller scope. Use it for pipeline and cash flow while the main site builds organic traffic.

**Profile headline to use:**
> Home organizer, declutter specialist & resale expert — Los Angeles. 14 years retail ops. I find the friction and fix it.

**Profile description to use:**
> I help people get their spaces back. Closets that stopped working, moves where the boxes are technically inside but nothing feels settled, storage units that have been closed for two years. I come in, find the friction, and make the space work around how you actually live. 14 years in retail operations at Nordstrom and Bonobos. I know what things are worth, how to move them, and how to make a space feel like yours again. Based in Los Angeles. Available for same-week bookings.

**Go to:** taskrabbit.com/become-a-tasker

### Thumbtack
**What it is:** Lead-generation platform for local service professionals. You pay per lead (not per job). Clients search, you get matched, you pay only when they contact you.

**Why it works for you:** High-intent clients. Someone searching "home organizer Los Angeles" on Thumbtack is ready to hire, not browsing. The platform has strong LA coverage.

**Rate:** Set a weekly budget cap of $20–$30 to start. Each lead costs $5–$15 depending on the job size. One booked job covers weeks of lead spend.

**Categories to list under:** Home Organizer · Moving Help · Decluttering · Estate Organization

**Profile headline:**
> Concierge home organization & resale — Los Angeles. First conversation free.

**Go to:** thumbtack.com/pro

## Tier 2 — Upwork and Fiverr (The Honest Assessment)

### Upwork
**What it is:** Freelance platform for remote professional services. Clients post projects, freelancers bid.

**Does it work for home organization?** Not directly — Upwork is remote-first, and home organization is in-person. However, there are adjacent services you can offer remotely that tie directly into your expertise and generate income without leaving your desk.

**What to offer on Upwork:**

| Service | Rate | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Virtual home organization consultation** | $75–$150/hr | You review photos/video of a space, provide a written plan and product recommendations. Client does the work. You provide the expertise. |
| **Resale research & valuation** | $50–$75/hr | Someone has items they want to sell and no idea what they're worth or where to list them. You research, value, and write the listings. Remote, scalable. |
| **Home inventory & catalog creation** | $100–$150/hr | Client sends photos. You build a documented catalog with descriptions, estimated values, and recommended next steps. This is your Legacy Catalog service, delivered remotely. |
| **Move planning & space layout consulting** | $75/hr | Client sends floor plan and photos. You provide a written room-by-room plan. |

**Profile headline for Upwork:**
> Home organization consultant & resale specialist — 14 years retail ops expertise

**Profile description for Upwork:**
> I help people figure out what they own, what it's worth, and what to do with it. 14 years in retail operations at Nordstrom, Bonobos, and SVP Worldwide — I know how to assess a space, value items across categories (apparel, furniture, collectibles, vintage), and build systems that hold. Available for virtual consultations, home inventory projects, resale research, and move planning. Based in Los Angeles. Response within 24 hours.

**Go to:** upwork.com/freelancers

---

### Fiverr
**What it is:** Gig-based freelance marketplace. You create fixed-price "gigs" that clients purchase directly.

**Does it work for you?** Yes — for the remote/digital versions of your services. Fiverr buyers are looking for fast, specific deliverables. The key is naming your gig precisely.

**Gigs to create:**

| Gig title | Price | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| **I will research and value your vintage or estate items for resale** | $45 (up to 5 items) / $85 (up to 15 items) | Written report: item description, estimated value, recommended platform, listing tips |
| **I will create a home inventory catalog from your photos** | $75 (up to 20 items) / $150 (full room) | Spreadsheet catalog with descriptions, condition notes, estimated values |
| **I will write your resale listings for eBay, Poshmark, or Chairish** | $35 (up to 5 listings) | Platform-optimized listing copy with title, description, keywords, pricing guidance |
| **I will give you a virtual home organization consultation** | $65 (45 min video call + written plan) | Video call review of your space + written action plan with product recommendations |

**Why Fiverr works here:** The resale research and listing-writing gigs are pure expertise — no travel, no minimum hours, scalable. You can do three of these in an evening and generate $100–$250. They also build your review count, which feeds the algorithm and brings more clients.

**Go to:** fiverr.com/selling

## Tier 3 — Consignment Platforms (Commission Income, No Upfront Cost)

These are not client-generation platforms — they are the platforms where you list and sell items on behalf of clients. You take a commission. The client pays nothing upfront.

| Platform | Best for | Your commission range | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chairish** | Furniture, vintage home goods, art | 30–40% | High-value items. Curated buyer base. |
| **The RealReal** | Designer clothing, accessories, jewelry | 30–55% | Luxury only. They authenticate. You drop off or they pick up. |
| **Poshmark** | Clothing, shoes, accessories | 20–30% | Fast-moving. Good for mid-range items. |
| **eBay** | Everything — furniture, collectibles, vintage, electronics | 20–35% | Highest reach. Best for unusual or niche items. |
| **Facebook Marketplace** | Furniture, local pickup items | 100% (no platform fee for local) | Fastest cash. No shipping. LA market is active. |
| **Vestiaire Collective** | High-end European and designer fashion | 30–40% | Slower but higher sale prices for the right items. |

**The consignment pitch (use this when texting people in your network):**
> Hey — I just launched a resale service. If you have clothes, furniture, or anything you've been meaning to sell, I handle everything — photos, listings, platform, shipping. Commission-based, no upfront cost. Text me a photo of anything you're thinking about and I'll tell you what it's worth and where to list it.

## Tier 4 — Professional Networks (Referral Pipeline)

These are not immediate income — they are the channels that bring the highest-value clients over time.

### LinkedIn
Your LinkedIn profile is your professional credibility layer. Realtors, estate attorneys, senior living advisors, and corporate HR professionals (who refer employees who are relocating) all use LinkedIn. A complete, specific profile with "home organization," "legacy catalog," and "resale consulting" in your headline will surface you in searches.

**Headline to use:**
> Home Organization · Legacy Catalog & Resale · Concierge Life Services · Los Angeles | The Well Lived Citizen Co

**About section (first 3 lines — what shows before "see more"):**
> I help people manage the physical side of life changes. Moves, storage units, a parent's home, the hidden value in things you already own. 14 years in retail operations — I know what things are worth and how to make spaces work.

### NAPO (National Association of Productivity and Organizing Professionals)
Free to join as a member. The directory is searchable by location. Clients who are specifically looking for a professional organizer search NAPO first.

**Go to:** napo.net/membership

### Houzz
Free business profile. Houzz is where homeowners go when they are renovating or reorganizing. Your profile shows up in "find a pro" searches for home organizers in Los Angeles.

**Go to:** houzz.com/pro
