## 5. Google Search Console — Do This After Launch

**Go to:** search.google.com/search-console

1. Add your property: thewelllivedcitizenco.com
2. Verify ownership via the DNS TXT record (your domain registrar — IONOS — is where you add it)
3. Submit your sitemap: https://thewelllivedcitizenco.com/sitemap.xml
4. Request indexing on the URL Inspection tool for your homepage

This tells Google your site exists and speeds up how quickly it appears in search results.

## 7. Well Lived Citizen LA — Secondary Brand Concept

This name has legs. Here is how it could work:

**Primary brand:** The Well Lived Citizen Co — the business entity, the website, the formal name on invoices and contracts.

**Secondary brand / social handle:** Well Lived Citizen LA — the Instagram handle, the hashtag, the neighborhood-facing identity. Warmer, more local, more conversational.

**Domain:** thewelllivedcitizenco.com (owned) + welllivedcitizenla.com (worth grabbing if available — check on Namecheap or IONOS)

**Use case:** When you are posting on Nextdoor or Instagram or handing someone a card at a Silver Lake coffee shop, "Well Lived Citizen LA" sounds like a place. Like something that belongs here. The formal name is for the contract. The LA name is for the conversation.
