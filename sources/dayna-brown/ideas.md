## Selected Design: Tactile Minimalism (Response 3)

**Why:** Dayna's brand is built on trust, precision, and warmth — not flash. The tactile minimalism approach delivers a site that feels like a well-made business card: confident, considered, and impossible to fake. The single-font system (Fraunces variable) gives editorial range without visual noise. The grain texture adds the warmth without the fussiness. The navy-on-cream palette is exactly what Dayna described — her signature colors, balanced and considered.

**What this means for every file:**
- Background is always warm cream `oklch(0.965 0.012 82)`
- Navy `oklch(0.22 0.07 238)` is the only color accent
- Fraunces is the only typeface
- Grain texture overlay on all cream sections
- Left-border navy lines for service descriptions
- Generous whitespace — minimum 80px section padding
- No gradients, no purple, no rounded-corner cards
