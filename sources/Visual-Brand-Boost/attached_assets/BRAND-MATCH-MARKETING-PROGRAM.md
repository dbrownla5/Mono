## THE THREE VOICES (and what each account is for)

### @thewelllivedcitizen — Company
- **Job:** positioning + lead gen for House Calls, Org/Modern Move, Legacy. People follow this account because they trust you, not because they want to buy a sweater.
- **Voice:** calm, observant, dry, slightly literary. Notices a thing, names it, doesn't moralize. No exclamation points. Em-dashes welcome. Short sentences mixed with one long one. Sentence case.
- **Forbidden:** hustle language ("DM to book!"), girlboss energy, before/after grids, stock photos, motivational quotes, emoji.
- **Allowed:** real moments, fragments, observations about modern life, the gap you fill.

### @thewelllivedcloset — Closet
- **Job:** drive sales (Poshmark/eBay) + intake (people sending you bags). This is the cash account.
- **Voice:** same register, slightly more connoisseur. Story-led, never bargain-led. The piece is a vehicle for a chapter. You're the eye and the steward, not the reseller.
- **Forbidden:** "OBO," "make me an offer," "must go," "DEAL," haul language, fast-fashion energy.
- **Allowed:** provenance, year, who wore it, why it matters, sizing in the same breath. Honesty about condition.

### @welldressedcitizen — Archive
- **Job:** one transition post, then dark. Catches your existing audience and forwards them.
- **Voice:** brief, warm, final. Two paragraphs max.

## VISUAL SYSTEM (the same across all three accounts)

- **Palette:** cream `#F5F0E8`, warm `#FAF7F2`, linen `#EDE7DB`, charcoal `#1C1917`, rust `#9A5B3C`. Nothing else. No bright colors, no white-white, no black-black.
- **Light:** natural daylight only. No flash. Shoot in the morning or late afternoon. North-facing window if you have one.
- **Backgrounds:** linen, raw wood, plaster wall, cream sheet. Never a busy room, never carpet, never your bed.
- **Composition:** one subject per image. Flat lay or hanger or in-context (the smoke detector, the labeled box, the binder). Lots of negative space.
- **Faces:** no client faces. Hands and objects only. You can show your own hands working.
- **Filters:** none. If you want, drop saturation 5–10% for a quieter look. That's it.
- **Format:** 4:5 portrait for feed posts (most real estate). Square only for grid coherence. Stories are 9:16.
- **Type on graphics:** Plus Jakarta Sans, charcoal on cream, lowercase or sentence case. Never all caps.

## CAPTION ARCHITECTURE

### Company posts — 3-part formula
1. **The moment** (1–3 sentences). Concrete, specific, no abstraction.
2. **The gap it points to** (1–2 sentences). Quietly names what's actually going on under the surface.
3. **The soft handoff** (1 line). Where to find you. No exclamation.

### Closet posts — 5-line spec
1. **The piece** — designer, year if known, what it is. One line.
2. **The story** — provenance, who wore it, where it lived. 1–3 sentences.
3. **The fit** — size, measurements, condition honestly stated.
4. **The platform** — Poshmark or eBay, link in bio.
5. **The cross-tag** — `@thewelllivedcitizen` at the end so the two accounts feed each other.

### Hashtags
- 3–5 max, never more. Mix:
  - one local (`#losangeles`, `#lavintage`)
  - one platform (`#poshmarkambassador`, `#ebayfinds`)
  - one niche (`#vintageysl`, `#consignmentla`)
- Never lifestyle-influencer hashtags (`#blessed`, `#girlboss`, `#entrepreneurlife`).

---

## WHAT TO NEVER DO (across all accounts)
- No before/afters. Your work is not a transformation product.
- No "DM to book." The site exists. Send people to the site.
- No begging language ("please share," "tag a friend," "save this post").
- No selfie content. You are the eye behind the work, not the influencer.
- No carousel posts longer than 4 slides.
- No reels with trending audio that isn't yours. Voice notes over silent footage > viral sounds.
- No emoji.
- Never post the same item across both accounts on the same day. Closet stays closet.

---

## CADENCE (sustainable, not heroic)
- **Closet:** 4–5x per week. Each new arrival = 1 feed post + 1 story. This is your sales engine.
- **Company:** 2–3x per week. One ethos post, one real-life moment, one service mention with story.
- **Stories on both:** daily if you can, even if it's just one frame.
- **Reels:** 1 per week per account, max. Don't chase. Use voice notes over object footage.
- **Posting times:** 9–10am or 6–8pm Pacific. Skip mid-afternoon.
