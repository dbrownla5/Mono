### Typography
- **Single font, everywhere:** **Plus Jakarta Sans** (free, Google Fonts).
- Weights you actually use: 300, 400, 500, 600, 700. Italic 300 + 400 for occasional emphasis.
- Don't introduce a second font. The whole brand reads as one voice partly *because* it sounds and looks like one voice.

### Photography rules (the "anti-Instagram" rule made operational)
- **Real spaces, real light.** Daylight if possible. Phone-quality is fine — preferred, even.
- **One object per photo** when telling a story. The clicker. The plate. The folded coat. Not flatlays.
- **Hands and small evidence.** Your hands packing, folding, holding. Backs of heads. No staged "founder portraits with arms crossed."
- **Imperfect is the brand.** A half-folded pile, a counter mid-process, a real "before." Don't sanitize.
- **Color tones to match the palette:** warm beige, cream, rust, charcoal. Avoid cold blues and high-saturation anything.

### Logo / wordmark
- Wordmark only: **The Well Lived Citizen** in Plus Jakarta Sans, weight 600, letter-spacing slightly open. Charcoal on cream. That's it. No icon. No flourishes.
