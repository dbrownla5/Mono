## ABOUT PAGE

### Founder statement (replaces the current block)
I spent years in retail operations — going into companies that were scaling, struggling, or somewhere in between, and figuring out what was actually broken. I could see it from the warehouse, from the shipping dock, from the budget, from the floor. I talked to CEOs and I talked to hourly employees and I was useful to both.

When I moved back to Los Angeles, I brought all of that with me — and applied it to something more personal. The homes people live in. The things they've accumulated. The transitions they're navigating alone because nobody in their life has the time, the knowledge, or the stomach for it.

I'm not a therapist. I'm not a contractor. I'm not an estate planner. I'm the person who shows up, listens to the whole story, and then figures out what actually needs to happen next. I can fix the drain, identify the fake Gucci, find the right buyer for the china, manage the vendor, and still be the most useful person in the room when the conversation gets hard.

I don't know what scares me anymore. I think that's the part that's useful to you.

— Dayna Brown, Founder
