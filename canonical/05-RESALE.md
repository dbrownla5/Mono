# 05 — Resale & Consignment

## Fast Bag Pickup — the flow (clothing & accessories ONLY)
1. **Fill a bag** — clothing, shoes, handbags, belts/small leather, scarves, hats, jewelry, watches. (No decor, furniture, art, books, kitchen, lamps, collectibles — those route to House Calls / Resale / Legacy.)
2. **Schedule a pickup** — free within LA service area (route stop-ins, building-desk drops, or direct bag shipment; 60–90 qt totes sized for an Uber courier). Most pickups in 2–5 days; urgent within 48 hrs.
3. **I evaluate after pickup** — piece by piece. Pickup ≠ acceptance; unsellable/damaged/counterfeit items may be declined.
4. **You get the list** — what's being listed, donated, or is a judgment call.
5. **Optional decision window** — weigh in, or hand full authority to Dayna.
6. **Sign the eDoc release at pickup** — items become WLC property until sold or the 6-month check-in.
7. **Listings go live** — routed to the right platform.
8. **Payout when items sell.**

## The paperwork — "Two signatures. That's it."
- **① Resale Agreement** — signed after evaluation. Commission, payout, routing live here.
- **② eDoc Release** — signed at pickup handoff. Items become WLC property until sold or the 6-month check-in; sets the decision-window option.

## Executed legal terms (wlc-resale-agreement.md — the legal lock)
- **Commission:** see `04-PRICING.md`; net of platform fees/shipping/approved costs; no upfront fees.
- **Payout (§6):** ⚠️ **conflict** — the executed agreement says **"first Monday after each 30-day period."** The live site says **"report by the 1st, payout by the 5th."** The **signed legal doc governs public claims** — align the site to it (see `DECISIONS.md`).
- **Inventory review (§9):** confirmation within 48 hrs of receipt; client has 48 hrs to raise discrepancies; then a **7–10 business-day** review.
- **Timelines (§10):** strong items ~90 days; real demand 6–9 months; **180-day** check-in (continue/return/donate/reroute); items unaddressed within 30 days of that notice may be cleared.
- **Retrieval (§11):** declined/paused items held briefly; retrieval within **14 days** of notice.
- **Liability (§7):** no consignment insurance; capped at net proceeds due; client warrants lawful ownership.
- **Authentication (§8):** good-faith reliance; no independent guarantee unless in writing.
- **Story/provenance (§12):** shared by default without client name; opt in/out in writing; contact info never sold.
- **Photography release (§13) · Tax (§14):** client is seller of record; no tax docs issued.
- **Termination (§15):** 7 days' written notice; unsold returned within 14 days.
- **Governing law (§16):** California, Los Angeles County; e-signatures binding.
