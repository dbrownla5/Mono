Pillar descriptions (verbatim, on-voice):
- **Home Org & Move:** "Your home, made to work for how you actually live. I come in, find the friction, and make the space work around how you move through your day." Reset modes: **You go, I work** (most common) · **Side by side** (sentimental spaces) · **The Closeout** (whole-home transitions).
- **House Calls:** "The person you used to have to call. Not crisis, not caregiving — the practical, unglamorous work of keeping a household running, handled by someone who actually likes figuring it out."
- **Legacy:** "Most families have a plan for the money. Far fewer have a plan for everything else. The decisions are yours. I handle the logistics." Deliverables: photographed inventory · written story/memory archive · resale/value assessment · dispersal strategy.
- **Resale:** "You fill the bag. I figure out the rest. You make one decision — *I don't want this anymore* — I make the next fifty."

## Fast Books — entry offers (rotational conversion layer, NOT permanent pillars)
- **Four-Hour Reset — $495 flat** — one room/task/outcome; the party-aftermath or "mother-in-law is coming" panic. (Lives under Home Org.)
- **House Call — $350** (= 2-hour minimum, packaged) — tech setup, errands, returns, the small stuff. (Lives under House Calls.)
- **Quick Resale / Fast Bag Pickup — commission-based** — hands-off clearing of the bags in your hallway. **CLOTHING & ACCESSORIES ONLY** (finalized). Household items route to House Calls / Resale / Legacy.
- **Move Wrap-Up / Closeout — scoped** — "You go first. We stay behind and finish the move. You land. We wrap."

## Structure
- **Pillars** = permanent relationship structures. **Fast Books** = conversion/routing tools, not permanent services.
- Three commercial tiers: **Entry** (Fast Books) → **Core** (org, moves, legacy, resale) → **Continuity** (retainers, flex blocks, ongoing resale).
- **Home Closeouts / Staged Transitions** sit under Home Org (NOT Legacy): "managed transition infrastructure, not passive storage." Billed $150/hr + resale commission.
