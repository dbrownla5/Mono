## What the company is
**Practical operational support for complicated modern life** — "one capable
person for the things that quietly pile up at home and in life: sorted, set up,
and handled." One number, one person, one invoice.
- Emotional translation: *"Someone competent is finally helping me carry this."*
- The differentiator: **the skill is judgment** — the act of evaluation itself
  (what stays, what goes, what's valuable, what's fake, what's worth saving). The
  services are "four doors into the same skill."
- Center of gravity (corrected 6/12): **NOT "transitions."** It is the
  **operational side of living**. "Seeing what's broken before it breaks people."

## What it is NOT (say it plainly — this is brand-critical)
Not caregiving · not medical/financial advice · not therapy · not cleaning · not
handyman work · not estate law · not forced minimalism / KonMari · not a luxury
concierge · not home organization as a category · not lifestyle curation · not
household "stewardship." **One person, not a company.**

## Founder background (do not misstate)
15+ years in **retail operations, logistics, warehouse management, product
development, executive support**; Poshmark Ambassador across 10+ resale
platforms. **NOT** a professional organizer, KonMari practitioner, cleaner,
