## Voice constants (never change)
1. **Emotional architecture:** notice reality → remove shame → introduce relief. Every page, post, policy.
2. **The Kitchen Rule (sacred):** *"If a sentence sounds like it belongs in a keynote, rewrite it until it sounds like something a trusted person would say in a kitchen."*
3. **Recognition over performance** — the reader thinks "Yes. Exactly," not "That's beautifully written."
4. **Observational specificity** beats elevated language (the dead smoke detector; the storage unit collecting procrastination tax; the printer that hates everyone).
5. **The judgment thread** — name the eye, not just the task, in every service.
6. Short, declarative sentences. *"I show up and get it done." "The decisions are yours. The logistics are mine." "One space, four hours, done."*
7. Never frame the business *only* around grief/crisis — "busy is as valid as grieving." No grief marketing; no therapy/estate-law claims.

**Tone:** confident without boastful · capable without clinical · personal without soft · light humor + slight exhaustion. Persona: *"capable woman with a label maker and good judgment enters while everyone else panics softly."* Register mascot: 🦝 **"brave little administrative raccoon"** — dry humor stays.

**The Dayna Test:** "What is the interesting thing here?" If the answer is "the marketing" → start over. If it's "the actual thing that happened" → continue.
