## The one rule above all others

**Move what exists. Invent nothing.** Do not build new features, businesses,
themes, or content. Do not claim something is "already built." Gather the correct
pieces that already exist; leave the drift behind. When unsure, stage it and ask
Dayna — never decide for her.
